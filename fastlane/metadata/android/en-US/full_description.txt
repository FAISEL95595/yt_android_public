YT Downloader Pro is an ad-free, fast, and powerful app for downloading videos and audio directly to your device.

Features:
* Smart Share: Download directly from the YouTube app via the share menu.
* Playlist Support: Select and download specific videos from entire playlists.
* Background downloading with progress notifications.
* Skip SSL errors for filtered networks.
* Built-in bot protection handling.
* Choose preferred formats and qualities (up to 4K/8K depending on the source).
